The Victorian Apiarists' Association

Page 6 · September 2026

The Association advocates to federal, state, and local governments to protect honeybee health, to improve Victoria’s honey resources and to ensure beekeepers’ interests are safeguarded and promoted. We work to secure effective legislation and encourage scientific investigation and research in all aspects of beekeeping.

Our achievements include:

• Introducing the Victorian Apiary Code of Practice allowing bees to be kept in urban areas. • Contributing to the new Apiculture on Public Lands Policy through our involvement with the Buzz Committee. • Working with Plant Health Australia which contributed to the development of the Australian Biosecurity Code of Practice. The VAA advocates for the beekeeping industry on behalf of all beekeepers in Victoria.

All beekeepers and interested groups are welcome to join the VAA. Membership benefits include:

• The VAA’s monthly magazine Australian Bee Journal (electronic version), plus one free classified advertisement per year. • Access to the members’ only section of the VAA website (www.vicbeekeepers.com.au). • Discounts for VAA events. • Access to individual beekeeper liability insurance with association discount. • Voting rights at VAA regional group meetings and the annual VAA state conference. • Opportunity to participate in and contribute to VAA sub-committees. • Opportunity to meet and share ideas with other beekeepers at local group meetings, field days, shows and conferences. • Opportunity to contribute to advocacy to government and development of policy on beekeeping.
