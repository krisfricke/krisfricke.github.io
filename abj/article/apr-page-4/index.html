The Victorian Apiarists’ Association Inc. (VAA)

The VAA was founded in 1892 and is the peak body for beekeeping in Victoria. The Association advocates to federal, state, and local governments to protect and improve Victoria’s honey resources and to ensure beekeepers’ interests are safeguarded and promoted.
