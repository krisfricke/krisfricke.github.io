VAA 2026 Annual Conference

Kris Fricke, John Kennedy · Page 13 · July 2026

By Kris Fricke

The 125 th Annual Victorian Apiarist Association Conference was recently held at the Novotel Hotel just beside Melbourne Airport.

It began on June 5 th with several presentations about our forests. This was a welcome change of pace for many as it sometimes feels like we only talk about varroa any more. Andrew McCallum talked about the work of the resources committee, botanist Chloe Foster presented on identifying Eucalypts, ecologist Karl Just presented about the counter-productiveness of planned burns, and forester Owen Bassett presented about Ironbark restoration. Altogether the presentations were very interesting and worked together emphasizing our role as beekeepers in advocating for good and sustainable forest management, and the fantastic work our friends and colleagues have been doing towards this end.

There were many other interesting presentations culminating in internationally renowned varroa researcher Dr Stephen Martin presenting on the ability of honey bees to develop their own resistance to varroa, informed by his 35 years or so of research on the subject around

the world.

At the gala dinner that evening a highlight was AHBIC chair Jon Lockwood running the auction. If the bees don't work out he could have a promising career in auctioneering or stand up comedy as he had everyone in stitches.

Saturday morning it was time to address varroa. Dr Martin presented again, about the impact of varroavectored viruses, and several other experts from our leading universities presented on topics related to varroa control. Saturday afternoon was split into two sessions, one industry focused and the other recreation focused. Industry heard presentations about nutrition, organic certification and emerging research, while a number of experts presented to the recreational beekeepers about things such as queen bees, mite treatments for small scale beekeepers, and many other things.

Altogether it was another great conference, and a great opportunity to see friends from around the state and catch up on all the very latest on every subject related to beekeeping.
