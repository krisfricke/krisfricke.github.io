Bendigo Branch Report

Stafford Kelly · Page 11 · July 2026

A number of Bendigo members attended the VAA Annual Conference. The event was a great effort by the VAA Executive Conference Committee and a really enjoyable occasion. It featured amazing international and domestic speakers. The dinner was excellent with bonus entertainment from the auctioneer.

It was quite an honour to witness Ken Gell present Ian and Robyn Cane with VAA life membership and hear the summary of the huge amount of work, effort, time and coordination put into the VAA to support Victorian beekeepers, amazing.

It was terrific to meet and chat with Elan Meyer, Victoria's Australian Youth Beekeeper, such an impressive and mature young man.

There was quite a variety in information from the speakers from the research world (United Kingdom, Southern Cross University, and University of Sydney), and industry, plus beekeeper educational presenters Lindsay Callaway and Stafford Kelly (see photos right).

Congratulations to the following Bendigo Branch members on their competition success: Richard Collins: Creamed honey, Whipped honey and Grand Champion honey; Scott Wright: Infused honey; Lindsay: photo competition; and Jocelyn our Honey judge repre-

sentative, great effort. I am looking forward to hearing all about the experience.

Overall, meeting and chatting with the researchers and beekeepers who are contributing to beekeeping, catching up with friends, and meeting other beekeepers for two whole days was my highlight, and the most enjoyable part of going to the conference.

Make and Mend is on Sunday the 19 th July, we are grateful to Angelique and Ross for hosting this activity, last year with great success and plenty of positive feedback. Stafford and others will demonstrate how to assemble, construct your own hive equipment and make pollen supplement. In late September the club will hold an activity day focusing on spring management and swarm prevention. Depending on the circumstances we will likely demonstrate a Demaree split or a double screen or both, check for details in early spring.

The Bendigo Branch Executive has commenced planning for the 45 th Annual Beekeeper Field Day to be held on Sunday the 11 th of October. The think tank is working diligently on the program implementing some new activities plus the traditional favourites. Our infamous Ross Blues will be putting the band back together.
