World’s expensive honey costs €10,000 per kg

What makes this honey from Turkey scientifically

unique?

Found in deep caves, Centauri honey can be found in

the Taurus Mountains of Turkey. This honey is harvest-

ed at around 2,500 meters above sea level. It is collect-

ed only once a year, far from human activity.

The honey forms over time inside rocky crevices,

enriched by minerals from the cave walls. This process

is rarely seen in conventional beekeeping.

fighting foams, and electronics, and persist in the envi-

Not so pretty but plenty of likes

A bee bandwagon effect may offset color disad-

vantages in securing flower pollinators

Kyoto University

If you were a bee, how would you choose a flower to

land on? You might go to the most beautiful one, as

pollination biologists have long suggested that flowers

with striking colors attract pollinators more easily. Or

perhaps, as other studies indicate, you might go where

other bees are feeding, like seeing a long line outside a

restaurant and thinking it must be worth the wait.

This raises the overlooked possibility of a floral

"bandwagon effect": that even a less striking flower

may attract as many pollinators as a brightly colored

one, simply by securing early visitors. A team of re-

searchers at Kyoto University was inspired to investi-

gate this prospect and its implications.
