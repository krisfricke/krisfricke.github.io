The Victorian Apiarists’ Association Inc. (VAA)

The VAA was founded in 1892

and is the peak body for

beekeeping in Victoria.

The Association advocates to

federal, state, and local

governments to protect and

improve Victoria’s honey

resources and to ensure

beekeepers’ interests are

safeguarded and promoted.

We work to secure effective

legislation and encourage

scientific investigation and

research in all aspects of

beekeeping.

Our achievements include:

• Introducing the Victorian

Apiary Code of Practice

allowing bees to be kept in

urban areas

• Contributing to the new

Apiculture on Public Lands

Policy through our

involvement with the Buzz

Committee

• Working with Plant Health

Australia which contributed to

the development of the

Australian Biosecurity Code of

Practice.

The VAA advocates for the

beekeeping industry on behalf of

all beekeepers in Victoria.

All beekeepers and interested

groups are welcome to join the

VAA.

Membership benefits include:

• The VAA’s monthly magazine

Australian Bee Journal (ABJ)

including one free classified ad

per year

• Access to the members’ only

section of the VAA website

www.vicbeekeepers.com.au

• Discounts for beekeeping

services and products, including

insurance

• Borrowing rights to the

extensive VAA library

• Voting rights at VAA regional

group meetings and the annual

VAA state conference

• Opportunity to participate in

and contribute to VAA

sub-committees

• Opportunity to meet and share

ideas with other beekeepers at

local group meetings, field days,

shows and conferences

• Opportunity to contribute to

advocacy to government and

development of policy on

beekeeping
