The Victorian Apiarists’ Association Inc. (VAA)

Founded in 1892, the VAA is the peak body for beekeeping in Victoria.

The Association advocates to federal, state, and local governments to protect honeybee health, to improve

Victoria’s honey resources and to ensure beekeepers’ interests are safeguarded and promoted. We work to

secure effective legislation and encourage scientific investigation and research in all aspects of beekeeping.

Our achievements include:

• Introducing the Victorian Apiary Code of Practice allowing bees to be kept in urban areas.

• Contributing to the new Apiculture on Public Lands Policy through our involvement with the Buzz Committee.

• Working with Plant Health Australia which contributed to the development of the Australian Biosecurity Code of Practice.

The VAA advocates for the beekeeping industry on behalf of all beekeepers in Victoria.
