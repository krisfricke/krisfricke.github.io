One home.

We are a strong, inclusive

member-based organisation

that supports all beekeepers

across Victoria.

We value community,

strong member

participation, trust,

transparency, and

respectful engagement.

We recognise branches,

regional associations, and

affiliates as essential to

the strength of the VAA.

We act with accountability,

collaboration, and integrity

in how we govern and

operate.

One voice.

We provide clear, credible,

and unified representation

to government, industry

and the community.

We advocate with clarity

and consistency on behalf

of Victorian beekeepers.

We lead with evidence,

professionalism, and

responsibility, building

confidence in our sector.

One guardian.

We protect honeybee

health, biosecurity, land

access and the long-term

sustainability of apiculture.

We support education,

standards, and best practice

to safeguard bees, honey

and pollination.

We act as responsible

custodians of the industry,

today and into the future.
