Biosecurity

reminders

Maintain clear

hive-group barriers

and avoid swapping

supers, frames, or

comb between these

hive groups.

Clean hive tools

between hives

or hive groups.

Carefully inspect

brood during

spring build-up

and investigate

any unusual brood

patterns or signs

of disease.

Recognising AFB: common signs

The following signs commonly occur in colonies affected by AFB,

although not every infected colony will display all of them.

■ Spotty or irregular

brood pattern, with

perforated brood

cappings, holes are

often off-centre.
