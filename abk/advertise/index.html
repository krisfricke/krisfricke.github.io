Pollination

Taking hives to almonds or other crops? These are the people to talk to.

Crop Pollination Association of Australia

The association of beekeepers who provide paid pollination services, mostly in the eastern states.

President: president.cpaa.aus@gmail.com
Secretary: jclyde.cpaa@gmail.com

ofi (Olam Food Ingredients)

Grows almonds on about 15,000 hectares, mostly in north-west Victoria, and brings in about 80,000 hives each year for pollination.

ofi in Australia: ask for the almond orchards’ pollination team.

Select Harvests

One of Australia’s largest almond growers, with orchards in Victoria, New South Wales and South Australia.

Contact Select Harvests: ask for the horticulture team about pollination.

About the ABK

The Australasian Beekeeper has been published for beekeepers since 1899.

Every issue is free to read on this site, page by page as it was laid out, and every article is also there as plain text. Nothing sits behind a login.

Subscribe (it costs nothing) and we’ll email you once a month, when the new issue is out. Subscribers can also look up their nearest beekeeping club, when it next meets, and their nearest supplier.
