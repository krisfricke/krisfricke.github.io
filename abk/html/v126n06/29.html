The experiments showed that vadescana was preventing

the production of mite offspring. In the two control

treatments, the non-targeting dsRNA and the sugar-based

solution without dsRNA treatment, there were on average

two female mite offspring in each cell. However, in both

treatments containing vadescana, they observed almost

no offspring. This could also explain the results in the field

where the control of the mite population lasts up to several

months. If the ability to produce mite offspring is blocked, it

would take quite a while for a population to recover.
