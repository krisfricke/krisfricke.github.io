for quantifying varroa resistance. Indeed, a low varroa

infestation can be explained by multiple phenomena either

linked to the environment, to beekeeping practices, to var-

roa biology or to a property of the honeybee colony itself.

(Boldface mine).

Back to the two questions that I began this section with:

1 Which selection criterion is most informative?

2 Which assessment method takes the least amount of time

and effort?

The answer to each is to perform mite wash counts, and

breed from the mothers of those colonies that control varroa

on their own.
