Practical application: I’ll give AI the last word. I wondered

whether artificial intelligence could solve the classic

beekeeping problem: “Ask ten bee- keepers a question,

and you’ll get fifteen different answers.” So, I of course

ran an experiment. I asked ChatGPT the question “Should I

insulate my beehive?” and got a very nice generic answer.

I then asked the same question twice again, and received

a completely different answer each time. Nope, AI didn’t

solve the problem. But the last paragraph of one of the

answers gave me hope (Figure 17).

If you’re uncertain, consider seeking advice

from experienced local beekeepers. They can

offer insights based on their experiences in

your specific climate and with the bee species

you’re keeping. They might provide guidance

on whether insulating your hive would be

beneficial and recommend the best methods or

materials for insulation in your area.
