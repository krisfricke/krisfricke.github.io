Varroa management plan centres education

The National Management Group for Varroa destructor has endorsed Stage

2 of the Transition to Management plan for Varroa mite.

The plan will focus on increasing training for beekeepers, with specialised

staff deployed nationwide to assist beekeepers, with $100 million budgeted

for the roll-out of the plan.

Australian Honey Bee Industry Council Chief Executive Officer Danny Le

Feurve said the plan was focused on education to ensure beekeepers could

understand and manage Varroa mite.

“We want to make sure that the beekeepers are comfortable in being able

to do the surveillance to find it and comfortable in what management options

they need to deploy, and how can they monitor and keep continual vigil

looking out for those pests,” Mr Le Feurve said.
