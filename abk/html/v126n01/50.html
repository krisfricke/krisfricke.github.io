The hive on left was treated with Formic Pro at Tocal

this March. This is one of just a few products that can be

used to treat mites whilst honey is on the hive. As with

any miticide please first read the label, withholding

periods, PPE and usage instructions thoroughly and pay

particular attention to hive ventilation and max-min

temperature requirements before using it. Ensure you

are also on top of your small hive beetles before using it

in warm seasons.

finding varroa mite in your hives please keep monitoring

regularly and treat when you must. If you have found below

treatment threshold mite numbers then don’t wait another

The main point to consider here is that at some point

over the next few years most colonies of bees (managed,

unmanaged and feral) within NSW will contain mites, so

managing them will become essential to those wanting

to keep healthy, productive colonies. At some point there

will also be interstate detections, so everyone needs to

thinking about a beekeeping future that contains varroa

in it. If you want to be a good beekeeper and maintain

strong hives and business then make sure that you are

doing your varroa monitoring frequently.

Also, if you know of beekeepers that are already

having to manage varroa in their bees then please do

not discriminate against them, as they were just quicker

to arrive to the very same spot where you will also be

at soon (nobody will be able to evade getting mites, so

don’t kid yourself about that). They have just had to

start spending the time and money required to manage

the mites quicker than you.
