Wheen Golden Bee

This past World Bee Day (May 20th) Wheen Bee Foundation CEO

Fiona Chambers was in Slovenia to accept the international Golden Bee

Award on behalf of the foundation. The award was personally presented

by the president of Slovenia, a country with very strong beekeeping

traditions that had spearheaded the adoption of International Bee Day.

The award is chosen by a committee of representatives of institutions

in Slovenia, chaired by the Minister of Agriculture, Forestry and Food,

and intended to “raise awareness of the importance of bees and

pollinators and encourages special achievements in their protection.“

16 nominees for the award were considered from Europe, Africa and

Australia.

Wheen Bee Foundation as described by the awards committee

“raises awareness of the importance of bees and other pollinators

for food security, biodiversity and ecosystem health on the smallest

continent, the foundation promotes and supports programs and

events to raise awareness of the importance of pollinator conservation.

It also provides funding for research, encourages the use of friendly

agricultural practices and develops planting to conserve natural

habitats and biodiversity for both bees and other pollinators.”

Wheen Bee Foundation joins previous winners Prof. Lucas Alejandro Garibaldi from Argentina (2021) for the promotion

of bees and other pollinators; president of the Slovenian Beekeepers’ Association Boštjan Noè (2022) for the preservation of

bees and other pollinators; and Serbian scientist David Davidoviæ (2023) for research on bees and other pollinators.

“It is important to move away from this idea that agriculture is the biggest threat to the environment, when it also has the

opportunity to be part of the solution.” Chambers notes.
