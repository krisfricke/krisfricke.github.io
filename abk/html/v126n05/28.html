WHAT DOES IT MEAN FOR

POLLINATION IN AUSTRALIA?

Feral bees

16-90% of feral bee colonies are

expected to die several years after

an area is affected. Populations

may eventually start to recover

once resistance evolves.

Commercial colonies

Small reduction (2-5%) in the

number of commercial colonies after

several years with acaracide use.

Long-term growth in commercial

colonies to meet pollination demand.

Pollination

Loss of feral bees will increase

demand for paid pollination. Loss

of commercial colonies will make

pollination services

more expensive.

Honey Production

Reduced honey production due to

treatment needs. Potentially some

increase in honey production due to

loss of feral bee populations.

Beekeepers

50-60% of beekeepers can be

expected to leave the industry,

mostly recrational beekeepers.

Costs

30% increase in cash costs to run

a beekeeping business. Additional

management costs of $35 per hive

every year. Losses up to $1.25 billion

to pollinator-reliant industries over

30 years.
