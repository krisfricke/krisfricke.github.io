Escaping Queen Question

When trying to mark my queen

recently, I ran into a problem that I

would like some answers to. As soon

as I tried to put my queen into the

marking cage, she took off out of the

hive. I’d like to know, if in the case

of this happening, does the queen

usually come back or is she gone now

for good? What should/can I do now?

*Anne

Ah, I think we’ve all been there.

Off she flutters, quickly vanishing

amidst all the buzzing bees, leaving

you standing there thinking “uh,

now what?” And there’s not really a

simple answer.

Obviously first you’re going to have

a look around for her to see if she

alights somewhere obvious, but then

you need to get on with things. But if

its in an apiary with other hives where

you’re probably going to be working

for a few hours, I’d come back to the

area periodically to have another look.

She can’t fly far and once she alights

other bees will join her, so finding her

again isn’t impossible. I once spotted

her on the truck wheel surrounded

by a classic circle of attendants (see

picture), other times I noticed a small

cluster of bees on a branch above the

hive and there she was, a few times

(most frequently I suppose) I found

her walking on the outside of the

hive, and one time I popped into a

shop just after losing a queen like this,

the shopkeeper said “oh hey there’s a

bee on you,” and my reaction is not

to swat bees but cup my hand over

it, which I did without looking, and

to my surprise when I stepped out

and opened my hand to release what

I assumed was a worker bee there

was the long beautiful carrot shaped

queen!

But oftentimes you won’t see her

again that day. Obviously if you

have one hive she’s more likely to

successfully make it home than

if you have 100 hives on pallets in

rows, but either way, if you lose

her all you can do is make a note in

your

logbook/journal/on-the-hive-

lid, whatever you do, reminding

yourself to look for her when you’re

back there. If I just had a hive or two

in my backyard I’d probably look in

the hive a week later to confirm her

presence. I think every time this has

happened to me she was back there

next time I checked the hive, so she

seems pretty well able to make it

back, but it’s certainly disconcerting

when it happens.

One time I lost, found, and lost

again, found again a queen. She

was a slippery one! I don’t habitually

name my queens but after that

I felt like she needed a name, and

off the top of my head came up

with Queen Vindaloo (I must have

had indian food recently)(and the

hive

consequently

became

hive

Vindolanda), and you, know it seems

whimsical, but I was running 500

hives at the time and every time I

came to hive 238 and looked at my

notes I was like ah Queen Vindaloo

how are you? And remembering our

little cat and amouse game made

her feel more like an individual

to me which broke the monotony

inspecting 500 hives on an endless

loop sometimes entails.
