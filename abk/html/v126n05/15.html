I didn’t want this venture to be all about

me, so I opened the doors to other local

beekeepers to supply honey as well. The

response has been fantastic. Sales have

met my expectations, and I’ve received a

flood of messages from both beekeepers

interested in participating and consumers

who love the idea. They think it’s neat,

and I couldn’t agree more.

What’s even better is how quickly and

efficiently the vending process works.

Within moments, customers can grab a

jar of fresh, local honey. We restock them

every Monday and Friday. The success has

encouraged me to consider rolling out a

couple more machines and seeing how

things progress over the next 12 months.

This is just the beginning of what I

hope will be a growing trend: making

healthy, natural products more accessible

through innovative methods. I’m excited

to see where this journey takes us and

am grateful to all the beekeepers and

customers who have supported this

initiative so far!
