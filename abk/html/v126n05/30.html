Conclusion

The honey bee and pollinator reliant industries are facing

an uncertain future due to the threat of varroa. While the

impacts are difficult to predict for now, these industries

can begin working together immediately to understand the

threat and plan strategies to bolster honey bee health. In

this way, we can ensure these industries stay resilient in the

face of varroa.
