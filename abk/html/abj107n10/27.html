OFFICIAL LABEL RANGE

ONE PAD

Temperature range (official label): 10–29.5°C.

Industry advice: if the temperature is over 25°C use the one-pad

application method. This method may not penetrate cappings, but it

prevents killing your bees in hot temperatures.

SPECIFICATION

HONEY SUPERS

Can be used with honey supers on (see withholding period).

WITHHOLDING PERIOD

Do not remove honey until at least two weeks after

treatment.

DOSE

Treatment is per hive. The dosage does not increase with

multiple brood boxes; the hive must have at least 6 frames

of bees.

CHEMICAL SAFETY

Schedule 5 – CAUTION

PPE

Do not touch with bare hands; wear chemical handling

gloves. A respirator is not required, but the fumes are very

unpleasant to inhale.
