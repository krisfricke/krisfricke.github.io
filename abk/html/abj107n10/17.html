The wider industry work

Alongside the immediate varroa pressures, work continues on

levy reform, including the ballot arrangements and communica-

tions needed to explain the proposal to beekeepers. There is still

preparation to complete before the industry is asked to vote. It is

important that people understand what is proposed and have a

fair opportunity to have their say. Take a deep dive on levy infor-

mation on our dedicated industry levy page: honeybee.org.au/

industry-hub/industrylevies.

We have also continued contributing to the review of the

Emergency Plant Pest Response Deed, the national agreement that

sets out how plant pest emergencies are managed and funded.

The experience of varroa needs to inform how future responses

work for our industry. Workforce discussions and our submission

on the classification of beekeeping occupations are another part

of this less visible work, aimed at ensuring the skills and needs of

beekeeping are properly recognised.

The Month Ahead

Over the coming month, our priorities will be following up

the Canberra discussions, progressing treatment access and the

roundtable actions, and getting the colony loss survey ready to

open. We will also continue the levy reform preparations and dis-

cussions with research partners about the work that will make the

greatest practical difference to beekeepers.

Thank you to the state associations, our Executive and the

many beekeepers who continue to share their experiences with

Bianca and I. That information helps us keep the national work

grounded in what is happening in the paddock. I hope spring pro-

vides some welcome opportunities, your bees build well, and you

travel safely through the busy months ahead. Please keep looking

out for each other.
