Bee colonies can also exist out in the open where you will also see the honeycomb.

What can you do if you suspect an established hive in a building structure?

Request help from a local beekeeper from the options listed previously.

Engage a Bee Removal Specialist such as Bee Removals Victoria - https://beeremovals.com.au/

Club members who collect swarms

A list of Geelong Beekeepers Club members who may be able to collect free-hanging and accessible swarms

in the Geelong district, with their areas and costs, is kept on the club website's Swarm Removal page. Most

do not do "cut outs".

For a beekeeper to determine their ability and capacity to assist you, it is helpful to provide the following in-

formation:

Location (including suburb and where the hive is living such as inside a wall, chimney, roof or type of

container such as compost bin)

Provide a photo if safe to do so

State how long the bees have been there

Contact details such as phone or email

Depending on the difficulty or distance travelled to remove the bees, it is not unreasonable for the

beekeeper to charge for their services. If it is an easy swarm removal, most beekeepers will provide a free

service. We recommend asking the beekeeper if a fee will be charged before accepting their assistance.
