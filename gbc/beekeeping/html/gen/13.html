Extractor Hire

The Geelong Beekeepers Club has two extractors available for hire to members only.

Extractor hire fees:

3 frame extractor $20.00 per FIVE (5) days + $50.00 deposit

2 frame extractor $10.00 per FIVE (5) days + $50.00 deposit.

One 3 frame extractor and one 2 frame extractor are located at freshwater Creek.

One lighter 2 frame extractor is located at Batesford.

Wire jigs

Wire jigs hire fees: $5 per week + $20 deposit (Located Batesford).

It is a condition of use that this equipment is returned in a clean, wax and honey free condition.

The Geelong Beekeepers Club accepts no responsibility or liability for the transmission of any disease

acquired by the use of this equipment.

Contact Stephen Fridey to arrange hire and collection - sfridey@icloud.com or use the form below to request

an extractor. We will contact you to confirm the booking and collection arrangements, or to arrange an

alternative date.
