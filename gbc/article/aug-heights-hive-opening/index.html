Heights hive opening

Page 16 · August 2026

Hi All

Our hive opening program at the Heights is about to commence for the coming season. This is where the club offers an opportunity to learn/participate in a hive opening with an experienced beekeepers. The club has 3 hives - a standard Langstroth, a Flow hive and a long Langstroth.

This year’s schedule is available on our website (outlines scheduled dates) with the first scheduled

opening booked for the Saturday 22nd August (pre spring opening).

We have scheduled a couple of openings to be focused on varroa mechanical techniques

Saturday 3rd October - Drone Capture and removal

Saturday 31st October - How to cage a queen.

We have a couple of family days where parents or grandparents can bring their children along.

These are Saturday 9th January and Saturday 6th February. These openings will schedule a bit later in the day so not to interfere with children sports. The usual time is 12.00 noon but please check the website for confirmed times when booking.

If you want get involved you can book online. Spots are limited.

Regards, Stephen Fridey
