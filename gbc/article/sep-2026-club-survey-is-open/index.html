2026 Club Survey is open

Page 6 · September 2026

Every two years, we pause and ask the real experts. . . you, our members, how we’re doing as a club.

Consider it a Bi‑Annual Health Check, and the survey is open now!

Your feedback shapes the future of the Geelong Beekeepers Club; from meeting nights and guest speakers to workshops, communication, fees, and Varroa confidence. This isn’t a box‑ticking exercise. It’s how we learn what’s working, what’s not, and where we can grow.

Past feedback has led to new workshops (Flow hive course is coming), better meeting formats, stronger biosecurity education, and more accessible resources. Varroa continues to reshape beekeeping across Victoria, your insights are more important than ever.

The survey covers:

Club meetings — nights, location, format, timing

Courses, workshops & guest speakers

Communication — newsletters, website, Facebook, member requests

Membership fees

Zones & local support

Varroa confidence and future planning

General suggestions and new ideas

It takes only a few minutes, but the impact lasts for years.

And as a thank‑you, members who choose to leave their name at the end will go into the draw to WIN a $100 Bunnings Gift Card. You’re also welcome to remain anonymous, every response counts equally. Your voice genuinely shapes the club.

Please take a moment to share your thoughts, your ideas, and your experiences. Together, we can keep building a club that supports, educates, and strengthens beekeepers across Geelong.
