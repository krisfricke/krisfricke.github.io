President's Comments

Nathan Whitford · Page 5 · September 2026

Dear Members,

Spring is finally here! What an unusual start to the season, sunny one day, raining and windy the next, and then a mix of everything in between. I’ve noticed a field of canola coming into bloom right next to my hives, which I’m hoping will give them a strong early-season boost. Once the weather settles, I’m keen to get in and do a proper inspection to see how they’re building up.

Geelong Show entries will have closed by now, and I’m hopeful we’ll see another strong club presence this year. It’s one of those small but important community activities that helps promote bees and beekeeping to the wider public. The club has again sponsored the honey competition, and I’m looking forward to seeing what our members produce.

We are still seeking volunteers for a few remaining shifts across our club commitments. If you’re able to help with the Geelong Show or Le Festival Français at The Heights, please check the club calendar even one shift makes a real difference.

Our September guest speaker is Dr Andrew Wootten (Master Beekeeper, VAA Vice President), who will be presenting on the Bawden Assay, an alternative to the alcohol wash. Developed by Trevor Bawden, this method offers a fast, cost‑effective and simple approach to Varroa and mite‑resistance sampling, requiring very little training. It uses Varroa infestation levels in capped brood as a key detection tool for queen breeders working on resistance traits. My own understanding of the assay is minimal, so I’m very much looking forward to Dr Wootten’s presentation.

If you haven't done so please complete the club Survey! it's an important health check for the club.

For members living out of town - you can now live steam the meeting.

It’s the beginning of a busy time of year for all of us. Keep up your Varroa monitoring, stay safe in the apiary, and all the best for the season ahead.

Nathan Whitford, President Geelong Beekeepers Club
