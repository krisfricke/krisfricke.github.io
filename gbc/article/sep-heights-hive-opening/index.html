Heights hive opening

Page 13 · September 2026

A new season has commence and our hive openings at the Heights have started but not the days scheduled due to our unpredictable weather.

This is one of the issues this time of the year with beekeeping finding a suitable day.

Our pre spring inspection scheduled on Saturday 22nd and our opening as part on the Beekeepers essential course on Saturday 5th September were both delayed due to inclement weather conditions.

The pre spring inspection was moved to Wednesday 26th August and the next opening was move to Wednesday 8th September.

Wednesday 26th August Stephen Davey and I took the opportunity to complete a quick pre spring inspection.

We found all 3 hive are progressing well

Langstroth Hive

One full 8 frame brood box a queen excluder, and an 8 frame ideal box. Hive strength – 6 frames of bees Food – There was nectar, pollen and honey in the ideal box. Eggs larvae and capped brood - yes and around 3 frames of brood Disease - No pest or disease found. Room - Yes the queen had room to lay. Queen was sighted Swarming – No queen cells There was a lot of condensation in the hive and a lot of ants in the top Perspex top. We remove the Perspex top and cleared away the ants. Very early days but progressing OK.

Flow Hive

One full 8 frame brood box a queen excluder and Ideal 8 frame honey super. Hive strength - 7 frames of bees Food - around 6 ideal frames of honey with nectar and pollen present. Eggs - eggs and larvae present and 4 capped brood. Disease - No pest or disease found. Completed an alcohol wash. No mites found Room - Yes the queen had room to lay Queen was sighted Swarming – No Again there was a lot of condensation in the hive Very early days but progressing OK.

Long Langstroth

One full 9 frame brood box. Hive strength - 6 frames of bees Food - food present around 1 full frames of honey with nectar and pollen. Will need to monitor. Eggs larvae and capped brood - yes and around 3 frames of brood. The queen was sighted Disease - No pest or disease found. Room - Yes the queen had room to lay Swarming - No signs of swarming. Hive is progressing well.

Wednesday 8th September again after quick view of the weather Stephen Davey and I again took the opportunity to complete a quick inspection. We found all 3 hive are progressing well. Not a lot of change from the previous inspections

Langstroth Hive

One full 8 frame brood box a queen excluder, and an 8 frame ideal box. Hive strength – 5 frames of bees Food – There was nectar, pollen and 5 ideal frames of honey in the ideal box. Eggs larvae and capped brood - yes and around 4 frames of brood Disease - No pest or disease found. Room - Yes the queen had room to lay. Queen was sighted Swarming – No queen cells We completed an alcohol wash and a drone uncapping with no mite found. Hive is strong and progressing well
