Preparation

Before getting started, you will need the following materials:

• A replacement brood chamber filled with clean frames of fresh foundation, floor

and entrance block, crown board and roof

• A framed queen excluder

• A rapid feeder with capacity for at least 4.5 litres of feed

• Foam strips or other material for blocking entrances

• Strong plastic bags or paper bags large enough to place combs/frames and parcel

or gaffer tape to seal them

• A queen cage (optional)

You will need to:

• Prepare 4 litres of strong sugar syrup by dissolving 1 kg of granulated white sugar

per 500 ml of warm water

• Ensure all equipment is clean before use

Method for shook swarming

Shook swarming is not recommended for

small colonies of less than 6 frames of bees. If

shaking a small colony is necessary, then

transfer them into a nucleus box.

Step 1. Move the hive that you intend to shake

a short distance away from its original site

Step 2. Place the clean floor on the original

hive site. Put the queen excluder over the

floor and then place the clean brood box

containing frames of foundation on top of the

queen excluder. Use an entrance block to

support the queen excluder if it sags at the

hive entrance on the original hive site
