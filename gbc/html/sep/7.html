Queen Breeding Course - Limited Spots

Queen Breeding, the basic Nuts and Bolts, with Bob

Brown - Beginner Workshop

If you're new to queen breeding, this workshop with Bob Brown offers a simple, practical

introduction to the craft. Bob will guide you through his own proven techniques, giving you the

foundation to develop your own queen breeding methods.

Host: Sue Moritz

Location: 40 Goggin Rd, Lethbridge

Available places: 4 members only

What's included:

One full day of theory and hands-on practice grafting larvae into cell builders

A share of caged virgin queens with assistant nurse bees to take home

Optional participation in other processes between the two sessions (eg.inspections, feeding,

keeping queen cells clear of wax and adding roller cages )

updating everyone via a WhatsApp group with comments and pics.

What to bring:

Strong head torch

Magnifying glasses (around 4× magnification, off-the-shelf)

Lunch

PPE for hive work

Schedule:

Day 1 - Sunday, 1st November

9:00 am - 12:00 pm: Theory and practice

12:00 pm - 12:30 pm: Lunch

12:30 pm - 3:00 pm: Grafting session

Day 2 - Sunday, 15th November

10:00 am - 11:00 am: Debrief and morning tea, then collect your queens
