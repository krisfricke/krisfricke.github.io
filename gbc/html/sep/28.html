United Fuel Discount

Fuelling Our Community

Thanks to the Geelong Beekeepers Club and the United Petroleum

Community Discount Card, you can now save on fuel and support our

club at the same time!

When you use the card at participating United service stations, you’ll

receive a 2 cents per litre discount, and an additional 2 cents per litre will

be donated to the Geelong Beekeepers Club Inc. It’s a simple way to

enjoy savings while helping our club thrive.

Who can use it?

Everyone! Members, friends, family, and the general public are all

welcome to take part. If you're on Facebook, please help us spread the

word by sharing the discount details from our forum page with your

network. The more people who use the card, the greater the rebate our

club receives.

This is a great example of passive fundraising—raising money through

everyday activities with minimal effort. Just by filling up your tank, you’re

helping us fund important initiatives and keep our club buzzing.

It’s free to join and only takes a minute.

So next time you’re at the bowser, remember: every litre counts. 

Simply click the weblink or scan the QR code to set up your discount

card today then download the United Petroleum App
