Volunteers needed for Geelong Show

The Geelong Show is one of the club's major events of the

year.

It takes a lot of time to set up, organise and staff over 4 days,

so if you are able to help out on one of the shifts, please go to

the club members area home page, and click the "Volunteers

needed for - Geelong Show" link and you will see the (red)

shift(s) that need filling. It doesn't matter if you're an

experienced beekeeper, or new to beekeeping . . you'll know

more about bees than the general public!

It's also a good opportunity to have in depth beekeeping conversations with other volunteers as

well as the public.

Our volunteers are provided a free club polo shirt as an incentive to volunteers . . and to keep things

uniform!

2026 Club Survey is open

Every two years, we pause and ask the real experts. . . you, our members, how

we’re doing as a club.

Consider it a Bi‑Annual Health Check, and the survey is open now!

Your feedback shapes the future of the Geelong Beekeepers Club; from meeting

nights and guest speakers to workshops, communication, fees, and Varroa

confidence. This isn’t a box‑ticking exercise. It’s how we learn what’s working,

what’s not, and where we can grow.

Past feedback has led to new workshops (Flow hive course is coming), better

meeting formats, stronger biosecurity education, and more accessible resources. Varroa continues

to reshape beekeeping across Victoria, your insights are more important than ever.

The survey covers:

Club meetings — nights, location, format, timing

Courses, workshops & guest speakers

Communication — newsletters, website, Facebook, member requests

Membership fees

Zones & local support

Varroa confidence and future planning

General suggestions and new ideas

It takes only a few minutes, but the impact lasts for years.

And as a thank‑you, members who choose to leave their name at the end will go into the draw to

WIN a $100 Bunnings Gift Card. You’re also welcome to remain anonymous,  every response

counts equally. Your voice genuinely shapes the club.  

Please take a moment to share your thoughts, your ideas, and your experiences. Together, we can

keep building a club that supports, educates, and strengthens beekeepers across Geelong.
