Volunteers needed for Geelong Show

The Geelong Show is one of the club's major events of

the year.

It takes a lot of time to set up, organise and staff over 4

days, so if you are able to help out on one of the shifts,

please go to the club calendar, pick your shift and hit

the [Volunteer now] button.

It doesn't matter if you're an experienced beekeeper, or

new to beekeeping . . you'll know more about bees than

the general public!

It's also a good opportunity to have in depth beekeeping

conversations with other volunteers as well as the public.

Our volunteers are provided a free club polo shirt as an incentive to volunteers . . and to keep things

uniform!
