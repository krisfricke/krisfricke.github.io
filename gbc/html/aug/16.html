h) Inspect this super seven to ten days after the introduction of the laying queen, or 12 days after a

queen cell was introduced to see if a laying queen is present.

i) Check that there are adequate honey stores. Feed if necessary.

Reference Aust. Beekeeping Guide - Chapter 6
